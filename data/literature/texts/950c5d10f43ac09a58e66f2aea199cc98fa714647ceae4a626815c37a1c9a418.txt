1.
 Introduction
l-Cysteine
 (l-Cys)
 is
 an
 important
 amino
 acid
 that
 plays
 crit-
ical
 role
 in
 a
 variety
 of
 main
 cellular
 functions
 such
 as
 protein
folding,
 metabolism,
 antioxidant
 defense
 [1]. Therefore,
 l-Cys
 has
been
 associated
 to
 a
 series
 of
 diseases
 in
 human
 body.
 For
 exam-
ple,
 l-Cys
 deﬁciency
 can
 result
 in
 liver
 damage,
 heart
 diseases,
skin
 lesions,
 hair
 depigmentation
 and
 sepsis.
 Meanwhile
 an
 ele-
vated
 level
 of
 l-Cys
 is
 associated
 with
 many
 diseases
 such
 as
 AIDS,
l-cystinuria
 and
 Parkinson’s
 diseases
 [2].
 Thus,
 the
 development
of
 suitable
 methods
 for
 the
 detection
 of
 l-Cys
 in
 various
 samples
has
 attracted
 great
 interest
 in
 recent
 years.
 A
 variety
 of
 detec-
tion
 techniques,
 such
 as
 high-performance
 liquid
 chromatography
[3], gas
 chromatography
 [4], mass
 spectrometry
 [5], electrochem-
istry
 [6],
 ﬂuorescence
 spectroscopy
 [7], chemiluminescence
 [8]
and
 capillary
 zone
 electrophoresis[9]
 have
 been
 reported
 for
 the
determination
 of
 l-Cys.
 However,
 these
 methods
 are
 complicated,
time-consuming,
 requiring
 sophisticated
 instruments
 and
 not
 suit-
able
 for
 routine
 analysis.
 Therefore,
 developing
 new
 methods
 for
the
 rapid
 and
 speciﬁc
 detection
 of
 l-Cys
 is
 of
 great
 interest.
To
 date,
 a
 variety
 of
 colorimetric
 [10], ﬂuorescent
 [11], chemi-
luminescent
 [12]
 and
 electrochemical
 [13]
 sensing
 systems
 for
l-Cys
 have
 been
 developed
 based
 on
 the
 interaction
 with
 metallic
nanomaterials.
 Among
 the
 different
 kinds
 of
 assays,
 colorimetric
detection
 of
 l-Cys
 is
 particularly
 attractive
 due
 to
 their
 simplicity

 low-cost.

BSA-Pt
