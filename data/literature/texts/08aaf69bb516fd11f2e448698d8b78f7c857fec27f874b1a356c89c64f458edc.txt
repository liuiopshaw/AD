Background: Researchers from Morgan State University (i.e., Dr.
Kadir Aslan) and Juxtopia (i.e., Dr. Jayfus Doswell) constructed

biological macromolecules of interest. APP is most commonly

of crystallization and microwave power; 2) Size of crystals;

PREBIOTICS FOR THE GUT MICROBIOTA
AS AN INTERVENTION FOR
ALZHEIMER’S DISEASE PREVENTION IN
APOE4 CARRIERS
Jared D. Hoffman, Sanders Brown Center on Aging, University of
Kentucky, Lexington, KY, USA. Contact e-mail: jdho253@g.uky.edu
Background: Alzheimer’s disease (AD) is the most common form
of dementia and a growing disease burden. Risk factors include
the APOE4 allele, Familial Alzheimer’s Disease (FAD) muta-
tions, and alterations in the gut microbiota, all potentially lead-
ing to increased amyloid-b (Ab) aggregation (Sci. Rep. 7:
41802). It has been suggested that preventative measures such
as a healthy diet may be a way in preventing AD for APOE4 car-
riers (Alzheimers Dement. 9(6): 657-65). Prebiotics are one po-
tential measure and are fermented into metabolites such as short
chain fatty acids, which have been demonstrated to improve
metabolic and brain health (Neurosci. Lett. 625: 56-63). We hy-
pothesized that modulating the gut microbiota with the prebiotic
inulin would prevent AD-like symptoms such as altered brain
metabolites and metabolic function in a mouse model using
either target-replacement APOE4 or APOE3 allele and 5xFAD
mutations (E4FAD or E3FAD). Methods: At 3 months of age
the mice were fed for 4 months with either control or inulin
diet. We utilized 16S rRNA ampliﬁcation and sequencing for
determining gut microbiota diversity and species variations,
and magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) for determining
metabolites in the hippocampus. Results: Our results indicate
that the prebiotic inulin led to a signiﬁcant difference in a- and
b-diversity of the gut microbiota along with signiﬁcant changes
in bacterial taxonomy in E4FAD mice. Further, the prebiotic
inulin increased scyllo-inositol, demonstrated to decrease Ab
aggregation (J Biol. Chem. 275: 18495-18502), in the hippocam-
pus of the brain in both E3FAD and E4FAD mice. Conclusions:
We suggest that prebiotics can modulate the gut microbiota lead-
ing to improved metabolic health, decreased Ab, and abolished
AD-like symptoms. Our approach in this study is translatable
and potentially an actionable preventative measure for APOE4
carriers.
