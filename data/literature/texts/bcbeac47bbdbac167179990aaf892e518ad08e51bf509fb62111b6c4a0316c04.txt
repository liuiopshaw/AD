ABSTRACT: We report the controlled synthesis and structures of two isomeric
gold nanoclusters, whose compositions are determined to be Au23(CCBut)15
(denoted as Au23-1 and Au23-2) by single-crystal X-ray diﬀraction and matrix-
assisted laser desorption ionization time-of-ﬂight mass spectrometry. This is the ﬁrst
time isomerism is discovered in alkynyl-protected gold nanoclusters. The metal-to-
ligand ratios in these two clusters are diﬀerent from known Aun(SR)m systems and
have not been observed in the Aux(CCPh)y family. This pair of isomers exhibits
diﬀerent optical properties, although they have similar structures and identical
components. For both Au23 clusters, time-dependent density functional theory
calculations revealed the frontier orbitals highest occupied molecular orbital
(HOMO)−1, HOMO, and lowest unoccupied molecular orbital (LUMO) are
mainly constructed from the Au15 kernel and V-shaped alkynyl−gold motifs. The
HOMO →LUMO transition of Au23-1 is optically forbidden, whereas it is allowed
in Au23-2. It is also found that Au23-2 cluster can be transformed to Au23-1
spontaneously under ambient conditions. This work oﬀers further insight into the synthesis and isomerism of all-alkynyl-protected
gold nanoclusters and will stimulate more investigation of isomeric metal nanoclusters.
■INTRODUCTION
Ligand-stabilized noble-metal nanoclusters have attracted
tremendous interest over the past decade because of their
unique physicochemical properties and potential applica-
tions.1−7 At this point, a series of structurally determined
nanoclusters with precise compositions have been re-
ported.8−35 Recently, special attention has been paid to using
gold nanocluster isomers as models for investigation of
structure−property relationships36−42 because these isomers
have identical compositions but diﬀerent structures. However,
examples of structural isomerism in the ﬁeld of gold
nanoclusters are rare because of the diﬃculties in structural
control of metal nanoclusters. In a strict sense, there are only
two pairs of isomers known in thiolated gold nanoclusters, with
compositions being Au38(PET)24 and Au42(TBBT)26 (PET =
phenylethanethiolate, TBBT =
4-tert-butylbenzene-
thiol).36,37,43,44 It was reported that two isomeric
Au38(PET)24 clusters exhibit distinctly diﬀerent catalytic
properties, and two isomeric Au42(TBBT)26 clusters show
various luminescence behaviors. In contrast, no isomeric gold
nanocluster has been observed in the alkynyl family, whereas
more and more alkynyl-protected gold nanoclusters have been
reported.23−25,45−47
Herein, we report the isolation and total structure
determination of two isomeric gold nanoclusters protected
solely by alkynyl ligands, namely, Au23(CCBut)15 (denoted
as Au23-1 and Au23-2). These two cluster isomers can be
separately prepared by diﬀerent methods. They have slightly
diﬀerent structures, but display totally distinct absorption
spectra. Interestingly, Au23-2 can be tranformed into Au23-1
spontaneously under ambient conditions. This is the ﬁrst
report of isomerism of gold nanoclusters protected by alkynyl
ligands.
■RESULTS AND DISCUSSION
Synthesis and Mass Spectrometry Analysis. Previously,
we obtained three gold nanoclusters including Au36(C
CPh)24, Au44(CCPh)28, and Na[Au25(CCAr)18] (Ar =
3,5-(CF3)2C6H3) by direct reduction of gold−alkynyl
precursors [AuCCR]n (Scheme 1, Route 1).45−47 In this
work, a one-pot method is used to synthesize tert-butylethynyl-
protected gold nanoclusters (Scheme 1, Route 2).
In the one-pot synthesis, Au23-1 was prepared by reduction
of Me2SAuCl and HCCBut/Et3N with NaBH4 in the mixed
solvent of chloroform and methanol, and Au23-2 was obtained
with a similar synthetic procedure in the presence of
tetraphenylphosphonium chloride (Ph4P·Cl) or tetrabutylam-
monium chloride (TBAC). It is noteworthy that the
introduction of Ph4P·Cl or TBAC into the synthesis promotes
the formation of Au23-2. We failed to obtain Au23-2 in the
absence of Ph4P·Cl or TBAC, but isolated Au23-1 instead.
These additives probably act as surfactants and facilitate the

Crystallization and Structure Analysis of the Au23(C
CBut)15. Single-crystal structural analysis49 and MALDI-MS
revealed that both Au23 clusters have identical compositions.
Each consists of 23 gold atoms and 15 tert-butylethynyl
ligands, but they have diﬀerent structures. As shown in Figure
2, Au23-1 and Au23-2 have a Au15 kernel and three V-shaped
alkynyl−gold motifs (tBu−CC−Au−CC−(But)−Au−
CC−But), two linear “tBu−CC−Au−CC−But” motifs
and two bridge −CC−But ligands. We can ﬁnd one alkynyl
bridge of Au23-1 capping on the triangle face 1 (Au3) (Figure
2a, pink) and one linear staple binding over triangle face 2
(Au3) (Figure 2a, blue). In Au23-2, it can be viewed as the
result of switching the positions of the linear staple and the
alkynyl bridge in Au23-1. Therefore, the diﬀerent arrangements
of the surface motifs account for the isomerism of two Au23
clusters, which is diﬀerent from the case of Au38(PET)24,
where completely diﬀerent metal cores are observed in the two
isomers.36
The anatomy of two Au23 structures is shown in Figure 3;
they have the same Au11 units, which can be viewed as the
fusion of three distorted octahedral Au6 units through sharing
ﬁve Au atoms (Figure 3a,b). The average Au···Au distance of
the Au11 unit is 2.923 Å for Au23-1 and 2.914 Å for Au23-2.
The Au11 unit connects four additional gold atoms to form the
Au15 kernel. Three of the four gold atoms (Figure 3c,d, sky
blue) have the same bonding environment in two Au23
clusters; however, the fourth gold atom has a diﬀerent
coordination (Figure 3c,d, Au1, blue). The Au···Au bond
lengths between these additional four gold atoms and the Au11
unit in Au23-1 range from 2.669 to 3.197 Å with an average of
2.822 Å, which is signiﬁcantly shorter than those within the
Au11 unit. For Au23-2, these values are in the range of 2.653−
3.089 Å with an average of 2.820 Å. Finally, the Au···Au
distances in the whole Au15 kernel give an average of 2.877 Å
for Au23-1 and 2.873 Å for Au23-2, which are shorter than
2.884 Å in bulk gold. The kernel of Au23-1 has C2 symmetry,
with the C2 axis passing through the Au2 atom and the center
of the Au3−Au4 bond (Figure 3c). The kernel of Au23-2 also
has C2 symmetry, with the C2 axis passing through Au5 atom
and the center of the Au6−Au7 bond (Figure 3d).
Furthermore, the Au15 kernels of both clusters are surrounded
by three tBu−CC−Au−CC−(But)−Au−CC−But di-
meric “staple” motifs (Figure 4, gray), two tBu−CC−Au−
CC−But motifs (Figure 4, red), and two simple tBu−CC
bridges (Figure 4, black). It is notable that the arrangement of

Previously reported all-alkynyl-protected gold nanoclusters
have the same metal-to-ligand ratios as Aun(SR)m, and they
have exact the same metal kernels as thiolated clusters.45−47 In
this work, the metal kernel and metal-to-ligand ratio of the title
clusters are diﬀerent from Aun(SR)m systems, and were not
observed in Aux(CCPh)y series as reported by Tsukuda et
al.50,51 It is worth mentioning that Jin et al. reported a thiolate-
protected Au23, [Au23(SR)16]−, containing a Au15 bipyramid
kernel capped by 16 cyclohexanethiol ligands.52 Alkynyl
ligands can adopt either σ- or π-coordination modes, while
the thiolate ligands only adopt σ-coordination (Scheme 2), and
this diﬀerence can dramatically aﬀect the structure. Although
the thiolated Au23 cluster and two title Au23 have the same
number of gold atoms, their structures are totally diﬀerent.
There are three V-shaped staples in title Au23 clusters, and
this motif was also found in the previously reported Au19 and
Au23.23,24 It can be seen that all aliphatic alkynyl ligands in
Au23-1 and Au23-2 adopt μ2-η1,η2 and μ3-η1,η1,η2 bridging
modes (Figure 4). The Au−Cα−Au angles in dimeric staples
range from 96.05° to 118.44° for Au23-1 and from 102.76° to
118.80° for Au23-2, respectively (Figure S1, Supporting
Information). Moreover, the average Au···Au distance within
the V-shaped staple in Au23-1 is 3.541 Å, which is comparable
with 3.563 Å in Au23-2 (Figure S1).
UV−Vis−NIR Absorption and Time-Dependent Den-
sity Functional Theory (TDDFT) Calculations. The optical
properties of these two clusters were measured and are shown
in Figure 5. Signiﬁcantly diﬀerent optical absorption spectra
demonstrate that the small change of the structure makes a big
diﬀerence. Au23-1 shows two distinct absorption bands at 598
and 412 nm, whereas Au23-2 displays two prominent peaks at
540 and 381 nm as well as a weak shoulder at 621 nm. The
absorption peaks in Au23-1 are slightly red-shifted compared to
those of Au23-2; no corresponding absorption at 621 nm in
Au23-1 is observed though. Therefore, these two clusters are
diﬀerently colored; that is, Au23-1 is brown−green and Au23-2
is reddish brown in CH2Cl2 (Figure 5, inset).
To understand the origin of the optical absorption of two
Au23, we carried out TDDFT calculations with the quantum
chemistry program Gaussian 09. Molecular structures from X-
ray structural determination were adopted as the calculation
model after structural optimizaion. The experimental and
theoretical Au−Au bond lengths of the two Au23 clusters are
listed in Tables S1 and S2. As shown in Figure 6, the
theoretical spectra agree with the experimental ones. For Au23-
1, absorption band I at 598 nm arises primarily from HOMO
→LUMO+1 and HOMO−1 →LUMO transitions, whereas
the lowest energy absorption band i at 621 nm in Au23-2 is
mostly contributed by HOMO →LUMO and HOMO−1 →
LUMO transitions (Figure 7). It is notable that the ﬁrst singlet
excitation (HOMO →LUMO) of Au23-1 is optically
forbidden (Table S3). For both clusters, HOMO−1,
HOMO, LUMO, and LUMO+1 are mainly constructed from
the Au15 kernel and the V-shaped gold−alkynyl motifs (Figure
S2, Table S4). There is a distinct peak ii at 540 nm in the
theoretical spectrum of Au23-2, which involves HOMO →
LUMO+2 and HOMO−1 →LUMO+2 transitions. In
addition, the higher-energy absorption bands around 400 nm
of two Au23 are primarily assigned to HOMO−2 →LUMO+2.
We also calculated the electronic structures and optical
properties of the two clusters using the ADF 2017 software
package and found that the results are consistent with the
calculations by Gaussian 09 (Figure S3, Table S5). According
to the Kohn−Sham molecular orbital energy levels diagram
and the analysis of atomic orbital character in the composition
of MOs of both Au23 clusters (Figure S4), the frontier
occupied orbitals are mainly constituted of Au (d and sp)
atomic orbitals, whereas the Au(sp) atomic orbitals constitute
the main contribution to the frontier unoccupied orbitals.
Accordingly, absorption band I in Au23-1 and the absorption
band i in Au23-2 are primarily attributed to mixed intraband
(sp →sp) and interband (d →sp) transitions. The band at
2.30 eV of Au23-2 (Figure S3, ii) also originates from sp →sp
and d →sp transitions. Notably, the deep occupied orbitals
(i.e., HOMO−14 of Au23-1 and HOMO−9 and HOMO−10
of Au23-2) of two Au23 have a large portion of C(p) character
of alkyne ligands, which greatly aﬀects their high-energy
absorption bands around 400 nm. It is worth mentioning that
thiolated Au23 is a nonsuperatomic cluster because its HOMO
is a hybrid orbital composed of gold atomic orbitals.52

Overall, the diagram of the frontier orbitals as well as the

Transformation. A transformation process was monitored
by UV−vis absorption spectroscopy (Figure 8). No change
was observed in the absorption spectrum of Au23-2 in the ﬁrst
2 h. Then the absorptions of 500−600 nm and below 400 nm
were attenuated, and the absorption between 400 and 500 nm
was enhanced gradually. After 7 h, the spectrum showed two
distinct peaks at 412 and 598 nm, and the solution color was
changed to brown-green from reddish brown (Figure S6). No
signiﬁcant changes in the absorption spectrum over the next 2
h were observed. Furthermore, we analyzed the products of the
structural conversion of Au23-2 using MALDI-TOF-MS.
Figure S7 shows two dominant peaks at 5665 and 5746 Da,
which correspond to Au23(CCBut)14 and Au23(CCBut)15,
respectively. Fragment species Au22(CCBut)15, Au24(C
CBut)15, and Au25(CCBut)16 are also observed, which were
formed under the conditions of the MS measurement. These
observations illustrate that Au23-2 transformed into Au23-1
without the formation of other sized nanoclusters. This process
of the isomerization was also observed by time-course nuclear
magnetic resonance (NMR) measurements (Figure S8). The
1H NMR spectrum of Au23-2 shows ten sets of protons in the
range of 1.0−1.5 ppm, corresponding to the tBu signals of tert-

It is notable that there is no obvious change in the UV
absorption of Au23-2 within 24 h at 4 °C (Figure S9a), which
indicates that Au23-2 is stable at lower temperature. In
addition, no decomposition was observed after the solution
of Au23-2 containing Ph4PCl had been stored for at least 24 h
under ambient conditions (Figure S9b).
In consideration of the structural similarity of this pair of
isomers, the transformation from Au23-2 to Au23-1 probably
involves changes only in the surface of the cluster (Figure
S10). However, the reverse transformation does not occur
because Au23-1 is more stable than Au23-2. Notably, the
experimentally measured optical gap of Au23-1 is slightly larger
than that of Au23-2 (Figure S11), which accounts for their
stability diﬀerence. In Gaussian calculations, the ﬁrst optically
allowed excitation of Au23-1 corresponds to the transition from
the HOMO−1 to the LUMO (2.04 eV), which is larger than
that of Au23-2 (1.83 eV) (Table S3). Moreover, DFT
calculations conﬁrm that Au23-1 is energetically favorable
over Au23-2 by 17.36 kJ/mol with B3LYP level (Figure S12).
Accordingly, the formation of Au23-2 can be attributed to
kinetic control, whereas Au23-1 is a thermodynamically stable
product.
■CONCLUSION
In summary, we report the synthesis and crystal structure
determination of a pair of all-alkynyl-protected gold nano-
cluster isomers. This is the ﬁrst report of isomerism in alkynyl-
protected gold nanoclusters. They are also the ﬁrst all-alkynyl-
protected gold nanoclusters with a metal-to-ligand ratio not
observed in gold−thiolate systems. These two Au23 isomers
exhibit distinctly diﬀerent optical properties. Furthermore, a
spontaneous transformation from Au23-2 to more stable Au23-
1 was observed. We expect that a variety of new alkynyl-
protected gold nanoclusters can be obtained with ﬁne-tuning
of the synthetic parameters, which provides valuable chances
for establishing structure−property relationships and studying
ligand eﬀects of metal nanoclusters.
■EXPERIMENTAL SECTION
Materials and Reagents. 3,3-Dimethyl-1-butyne (tBuCCH,
98%) was purchased from J&K. Tetraphenyl phosphonium chloride
(Ph4P·Cl, >98%) and tetrabutyl ammonium chloride (TBAC, 98%)
were purchased from TCI. Sodium borohydride (NaBH4, 98%) and
other reagents employed were purchased from Sinopharm Chemical
Reagent Co. Ltd. (Shanghai, China). All reagents were used as
received without further puriﬁcation. Me2SAuCl was prepared
according to the literature method.53
Synthesis of Au23-1. HCCBut (1 mmol, 123 uL) was added to
a 30 mL chloroform/methanol (5:1) solution containing Me2SAuCl
(147.5 mg, 0.5 mmol) under vigorous stirring, which was followed by
the addition of trimethylamine (100 μL). Then a freshly prepared 5
mL EtOH solution of NaBH4 (0.15 mmol) was added dropwise to the
above solution. The color changed from colorless to yellow and ﬁnally
dark brown. The reaction continued for 20 h at room temperature in
the dark. Then the solvents were removed by rotary evaporation. The
obtained product was washed with methanol and was dissolved in
CH2Cl2. After centrifugation, the supernatant solution was divided
into eight parts and each part was subjected to the diﬀusion of
acetonitrile at 4 °C to aﬀord sheet dark crystals in 2 weeks in 31%
yield (37.9 mg, based on Au).
Synthesis of Au23-2. The synthesis Au23-2 is similar to that of
Au23-1 and the synthetic procedures are identical until the addition of
NaBH4. Then 5 mL of MeOH solution containing Ph4P·Cl (0.1
mmol) was added. The color changed from colorless to yellow and
ﬁnally to dark brown. The reaction continued for 20 h at room
temperature in the dark. The workup is also the same as that for Au23-
1. Block dark crystals was obtained at 4 °C within 2 weeks in 10%
yield (13.2 mg, based on Au).
